## Upscale in the right order

Identify the source problem first. Use deblocking for compression, light denoise for unstable noise, and deinterlace before upscaling. Delay frame interpolation until cadence and duplicate frames are understood.

Apply a moderate 2x upscale and compare it with a high-quality resize. Sharpen only enough to clarify edges. If denoise looks sterile, stable finishing grain can restore texture.

Each stage can amplify the previous stage’s mistake. A heavily sharpened source gives the AI ringing to magnify. Strong face recovery may invent eyes or teeth. Frame interpolation can turn an already unstable edge into a moving smear.

## Fix waxy faces halos and flicker

For waxy faces, reduce face recovery and denoise, then restore light grain. For bright halos, lower sharpening and use a gentler model. For flicker, choose a method that uses temporal information, reduce enhancement strength, and inspect whether the source itself contains exposure or compression changes.

When small details change identity from frame to frame, the model is inventing more than it can stabilize. Lower the target scale or accept a softer result. A stable, slightly soft face looks more real than a sharp face that rearranges itself during speech.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best AI video upscaler for 4K

It depends on the source and workflow. Compare a browser option with Topaz, HitPaw VikPea, Aiarty, Resolve, or UniFab using the same moving test. A 4K label does not guarantee credible detail.

### Can AI restore missing video detail

AI can predict plausible detail and reduce visible damage. It cannot prove what was present in information the camera never captured.

### Why does an upscaled video flicker

Frame-by-frame changes in generated texture cause flicker. Strong enhancement, noise, compression, and fast motion make the problem worse.

### What is the best free AI video upscaler

The best free option is one that lets you download a useful result with acceptable privacy and no unexpected watermark. Verify current limits before choosing.

### Should I denoise before or after upscaling

Remove disruptive noise or compression before upscaling, but keep the pass gentle. Add stable finishing grain after the upscale if the image looks plastic.
